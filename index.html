A little help

Frequently asked questions.

Can I order a custom cake?

Yes, this concept includes a custom cake enquiry form. A real bakery would confirm availability, pricing, design details and collection arrangements with the customer.

How far in advance should I enquire?

For a real bakery, advance notice depends on the cake's size and complexity. Customers should confirm the bakery's current lead times before placing an order.

Do you offer dietary-friendly options?

A real bakery should clearly list verified dietary options and discuss allergens directly with customers. This demo does not make any allergy or dietary guarantees.

Can I buy pastries without ordering ahead?

This would depend on the bakery's stock and opening hours. A live business website should publish its current hours and contact details.
